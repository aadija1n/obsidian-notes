
| Divisible by | Criteria                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2            | when unit place is 0 or divisible by 2.                                                                                            |
| 3            | when the sum of digits is divisible by 3.                                                                                          |
| 4            | when last 2 digits are 0s or divisible by 4                                                                                        |
| 5            | when unit digit is 5 or 0                                                                                                          |
| 6            | when the number is divisible by both 2 and 3                                                                                       |
| 8            | if last three digits are 0's or divisible by 8                                                                                     |
| 9            | if sum of digits is divisible by 9                                                                                                 |
| 10           | when unit place is 0                                                                                                               |
| 11           | when difference between sum of digits at odd places and sum of digits at even places is divisible by 11.                           |
| 7 and 13     | divide the number in group of 3 from left side. and then sum of odd places - even places. the result must be divisible by 7 or 13. |

To check divisibility by exponents of 2, for every exponent increase number of last digits increase :
- 2<sup>1</sup> = 2 : last digit 0 or divisible by 2
- 2<sup>2</sup> = 4 : last 2 digits 0's or divisible by 4
- 2<sup>3</sup> = 8 : last 3 digits 0's or divisible by 8
- 2<sup>4</sup> = 16 ; last 4 digits 0's or divisible by 16
- and so on

Same is the case with exponents of 10 :
- 10<sup>1</sup> = 10 : last 1 digit must be 0
- 10<sup>2</sup> = 100 : last 2 digits must be 0
- 10<sup>3</sup> = 1000 : last 3 digits must be 0
- and so on

**Note** : if any given number is divisible by co-prime factors of 10, the number is divisible by 10. e.g. 10 = 5 x 2 (5 and 2 are co-prime), any number divisible by both 5 and 2 - will be divisible by 10. similarly applicable to all numbers, but the only condition is that factors must co-prime.
- 6 = 3 x 2
- 10 = 5 x 2
- 15 = 5 x 3
- 18 = 6 x 3 ❌ 9 x 2 ✅ (must be co-primes)
- 30 = 2 x 3 x 5
- and so on

**Q.** how many natural numbers less than 1000 are divisible by 5 or 7, but not 35.
- (A) 312
- (B) 313
- (C) 314
- (D) 315

<details><summary>Click to see answer</summary>Correct option : B<br>
numbers divisible by 5 less than 1000 (i.e. till 999) : 999 / 5 = 199 (ignore decimal values)<br>
for 7 : 999 / 7 = 142<br>
for 35 : 999 / 35 = 28<br>
(199 + 142) - (28) = 313
</details>
